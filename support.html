I found a wrong answer

Please tell us. Traffic law changes and differs by state, and we would rather fix an error than leave it in. Email us with the question text and your state, and we will check it against the official handbook.

That said, please read our Terms of Use: this app is an unofficial study aid, we do not guarantee that any content is accurate or current, and you should always verify a rule in your state's official driver handbook before relying on it.

My progress disappeared

The app has no account and no cloud backup — everything is stored on your device only. Progress does not transfer to a new phone, and reinstalling the app clears it. This is deliberate: it means we never collect or hold any of your data.

I paid for Premium but still see ads

Open the app, go to Profile → Premium and use Restore purchase. Make sure you are signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account you bought it with. Subscriptions are tied to that store account, not to the app.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed entirely by your app store. On iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid renewal. Refunds are handled by the store under its own rules; we cannot issue them.

My state isn't listed

We currently cover California, Texas, Florida, New York, Illinois and Pennsylvania. Road-sign questions are federal and apply everywhere, so they are useful in any state.
