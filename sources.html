Official sources

Every rule, figure and road sign in the DMV Practice Test app comes from one of the documents below.

We are not the DMV. DMV Practice Test is an independent study app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any Department of Motor Vehicles, any state agency or any government body. We cannot issue a permit, a licence or an appointment, and we do not process any official application — only your state agency can do that.

Each entry links to the publishing agency's own page. Some state government servers refuse connections from outside the United States, so a link may fail to load on your network even though the page is live; an archived copy is listed underneath for that case. The agency's own page is always the authority.
