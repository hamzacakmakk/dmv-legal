Official sources

Every rule, figure and road sign in the DMV Practice Test app comes from one of the documents below.

We are not the DMV. DMV Practice Test is an independent study app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any Department of Motor Vehicles, any state agency or any government body. We cannot issue a permit, a licence or an appointment, and we do not process any official application — only your state agency can do that.

Each entry links to the publishing agency's own page. Some state government servers refuse connections from outside the United States, so a link may fail to load on your network even though the page is live; an archived copy is listed underneath for that case. The agency's own page is always the authority.

Road signs — all 50 states

  • Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices (MUTCD), 11th Edition

    Published by U.S. Federal Highway Administration

    Used for: Sign shapes, colours and meanings, and every road-sign question. The federal standard applies in all 50 states.

    https://mutcd.fhwa.dot.gov/kno_11th_Edition.htm

    If that address does not load from your country or network: archived copy

  • MUTCD sign artwork on Wikimedia Commons

    Published by Wikimedia Commons — files created by the U.S. Federal Highway Administration

    Used for: The sign images themselves. The designs are the public domain MUTCD drawings published by the FHWA; this is the file archive we download them from. It is not the source of any rule.

    https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:MUTCD

    If that address does not load from your country or network: archived copy
